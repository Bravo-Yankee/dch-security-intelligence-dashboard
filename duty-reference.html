Enforcement ladder

The standard path from first observation to formal penalty, per the Community Manager's authority under §3 and §11.

At the Community Manager's discretion — not mandatory before a Notice of Violation. §11.iii

§2–3, 11

Enforcement Process

6 items

How a breach turns into paperwork, and who has authority to act on the spot.

  • §2Notice of Violation (NOV): formal notice of a breach, issued via the Mollak System or directly by the Community Manager.
  • §2Violation Penalty: the consequence when an NOV isn't corrected — usually a fine, sometimes reporting to Local Authorities.
  • §3.ivIf a breach threatens persons or property, the Community Manager is authorised to enforce immediately — no waiting period required.
  • §3.vThe Community Manager may pursue a breach until corrected, including initiating legal proceedings.
  • §11.iiiA verbal or written warning ahead of a formal NOV is discretionary, not required.
  • §11.v–viFines are charged to the Owner's Service Fee account; legal costs from non-compliance are borne by the responsible party.
§4.1–4.2

Noise & Privacy

6 items
  • §4.1.i–iiNoise is deemed excessive if it's audible in a neighbouring Unit with windows and doors closed.
  • §4.1.viiCleaning equipment / power tools only permitted 9:00–13:00 and 15:00–17:00; contractor-permit work must keep to this window too.
  • §4.1.viResidents are responsible for children's noise in corridors, lobbies, parking areas and other Common Areas.
  • §4.1.viiiEscalation path: affected Resident → Community Security intervenes → unresolved → Community Manager → unresolved → Local Authority directly. Noise from outside the Community is a Local Authority matter throughout.
  • §4.2.ivCCTV or surveillance equipment directed outside a private property, or facing another Unit / Common Area, is strictly prohibited.
  • §4.2.iiInvitees must not create disturbances, operate drones, or otherwise disrupt a Resident's privacy or peaceful enjoyment.
§4.3, 4.8–4.9

Conduct, Use & Household Staff

8 items

Community staff

  • §4.3.iVerbal or physical abuse, or disrespectful conduct, toward Community staff is a serious violation of the Rules.
  • §4.3.ii–iiiComplaints about staff mistreatment go in writing to the Community Manager; offenders receive an NOV and possible penalty.

Unit use

  • §4.8.iResidential use only, single-family occupancy: Owners, Tenants, immediate family, guests and domestic employees — per the Agreement of Sale.
  • §4.8.iiCompanies may not house bachelor employees in a Unit shared with other bachelors.
  • §4.8.iii, vNo public-access commercial activity without written approval; no partitioning a Unit to let out individual rooms.
  • §4.8.xiUnits and Common Areas may not be used for illegal or immoral activity, or anything that could harm the Community's reputation.

Household staff

  • §4.9.iAll household staff must hold a valid Dubai Immigration residence visa.
  • §4.9.iiHousing staff not sponsored by the Owner/Tenant risks criminal prosecution. escalate
§4.4

Pets & Animals

9 items
  • §4.4.i–iiOnly domestic pets — dogs, cats, birds, fish, reptiles; no commercial breeding, no unreasonable quantities.
  • §4.4.ivAll animals, including reptiles, must be approved by Dubai Municipality; check the Emirate's banned-pets list.
  • §4.4.x–xiDogs on a leash with a competent handler at all times outside the Unit; all pets need visible ID outdoors.
  • §4.4.xiiIn lifts without a dedicated pet elevator, pet owners must yield to Residents without pets.
  • §4.4.xiiiHandler must clean up faeces/urine immediately — non-compliance is an NOV plus penalty.
  • §4.4.xviiPets barred from parking areas, children's play areas, BBQ areas, podiums, walkways and most Shared Facilities — except designated dog parks.
  • §4.4.xviiiFeeding stray animals is strictly prohibited under Dubai Municipality rules.
  • §4.4.xixLost/abandoned pets, cruelty, pet attacks — direct the resident to Dubai Municipality, not just the Community Manager. escalate
§4.5

Hazardous Activities & Fire Safety

11 items

Whole section Any breach here results in an immediate Notice of Violation and penalty on the first occurrence — no verbal warning step. §4.5.xx

  • §4.5.iFirearms, hunting, trapping, and toy/air guns capable of harm are strictly prohibited.
  • §4.5.ii–ivOpen fires only in an attended BBQ unit; no cooking or grilling of any kind on a balcony or terrace.
  • §4.5.v, xiNo smoking (cigarettes, shisha) on balconies, in hallways, stairwells or Shared Facilities — designated areas only.
  • §4.5.ixNo storing or using flammable chemicals/liquids/gases without the Community Manager's written consent (a vehicle's own fuel tank is exempt).
  • §4.5.xDrones and fireworks are strictly prohibited anywhere in the Community, including on balconies.
  • §4.5.xii–xiiiNo interference with fire safety devices; Residents must grant access for life-safety system inspections.
  • §4.5.xiv–xvNo activity that could trigger a false alarm; strict compliance with evacuation plans and drills.
  • §4.5.xviImproper disposal of live coal, lit cigarettes, flammable chemicals or batteries in Common Areas is prohibited.
  • §4.5.xviiLPG cylinders banned unless the Unit is specifically designed and approved for one — max 1 cylinder even then.
  • §4.5.xviiiOnly an authorised provider may activate a central gas connection — never the Resident or an unapproved technician.
§4.6–4.7

Waste & Vandalism

7 items

Waste

  • §4.6.i–iiHousehold waste goes in the designated refuse room only — no dumping in Common Areas.
  • §4.6.vi–viiRecycling is encouraged; contaminated recyclables are rejected and must go to general waste.
  • §4.6.ixHazardous waste (batteries, electronics, appliances) follows Dubai Municipality's hazardous-waste procedure — not the regular bins.
  • §4.6.xiNon-compliance after a Security approach earns an NOV; persistent cases let the Community Manager remove items at the Resident's expense.

Vandalism

  • §4.7.i–iiLittering, graffiti and vandalism are strictly prohibited; reinstatement cost is charged to the individual responsible, or recovered via Service Charges if unidentified.
  • §4.7.iiiSerious vandalism is reported to the Local Authorities for further action. escalate
  • §4.7.vNo marking, painting, or driving nails/screws into Common Area structures without written consent.
§5.1–5.6

Shared Facilities & Access

19 items

General — §5.1

  • §5.1.iiiChildren under 14 always need supervision by an adult 18+.
  • §5.1.iv–vPets barred except in designated dog parks; alcohol strictly prohibited in all Shared Facilities and Common Areas.
  • §5.1.viiPrivate functions need the Community Manager's written permission and may require a security deposit.
  • §5.1.viiiSerious injury or death is reported to the relevant Local Authority. escalate
  • §5.1.xviiiRepeat or severe violations can mean permanent exclusion from Shared Facilities.
  • MCR §3.1.6Villa Master Community parks: private functions capped at 8 guests by default, unless the Community Manager approves more — Vertical Communities rules don't set a fixed number.

Sports areas — §5.2

  • §5.2.vii–xPlay-time caps when others are waiting: basketball/tennis 60 min; 3+ playing basketball 90 min; tennis pairs 45 min; doubles tennis 2 hrs.

Gymnasiums — §5.3

  • §5.3.iAccess limited to Residents and Invitees staying 24+ hrs in the Unit; max 1 Invitee per Resident without approval.
  • §5.3.iiiWeight equipment: 16+ only; ages 14–15 need active adult supervision; 13 and under strictly prohibited, even supervised.
  • §5.3.xxiiPersonal training in gym/pool must be booked through the Emaar One app; trainers need a valid NOC.

Swimming pools — §5.4

  • §5.4.xviiSame 24-hour-stay access rule as the gym; Invitees must always be accompanied.
  • §5.4.xii–xiiiNo alcohol, glass, or food/drink other than bottled water in the pool area.
  • §5.4.viiShower required before using the pool or jacuzzi.

Community beaches — §5.5

  • §5.5.ii–iiiNo swimming after sunset; beaches are unmanned — entering the water is entirely at the swimmer's own risk.
  • §5.5.iv.aRed flag: no swimming. Yellow flag: swim with caution.
  • §5.5.iv.e–hNo alcohol, smoking, camping, overnight sleeping, fires or BBQs; children under 12 need a responsible adult present.

Utility / plant rooms — §5.6

  • §5.6.iService plant, filtration and telephone rooms are off-limits to unauthorised persons — access only for designated maintenance personnel.
§6.1–6.7

Access, Parking & Roads

17 items

Building access — §6.1

  • §6.1.i–iiValid access card or ANPR registration required; no tailgating or using the exit driveway to gain entry.
  • §6.1.viContractors and handymen need Community Manager–approved entry permits — access can be denied at any time.
  • §6.1.viiiHoliday Home Operators must be physically present for guest check-in and check-out.

Parking — §6.2

  • §6.2.vVisitor bays: max 4 hrs, first-come-first-served, no overnight parking.
  • §6.2.xvCar park speed limit: 10 km/h.
  • §6.2.xiPeople-of-Determination bays require a valid POD permit.
  • §6.2.xviiOil/fluid spills must be cleaned within 24 hrs or the Community Manager arranges cleanup and recovers the cost.
  • §6.2.xxii–xxvEV chargers: active charging only, max 4 hrs, then vacate. Misuse risks towing plus an NOV.

Roads & e-scooters — §6.3–6.4

  • §6.3.iSpeed limits: 40 km/h main roads, 25 km/h neighbourhood roads — posted signage always governs.
  • §6.3.iiiDirt bikes, trail bikes, sand buggies and unlicensed motorised vehicles/EVs are strictly prohibited.
  • §6.4.i–ivE-scooter riders: 16+ with a valid RTA permit, helmet mandatory, 20 km/h max speed.
  • §6.4.xiiRental e-scooter/e-bike services are not permitted in gated communities — RTA-designated areas only.

Deliveries & removals — §6.7

  • §6.7.ii, viiiBulk deliveries/removals need 48 hrs advance approval and are permitted Mon–Sat, 10:00–18:00 only — never on public holidays.
  • §6.7.ivFurniture and bulky items must use designated service elevators — passenger lifts need written consent.
§7.1–7.11

Unit & Building Upkeep

10 items
  • §7.1.i–iiUnoccupied Units must be regularly inspected/treated for pests; report anything beyond ants, non-poisonous spiders, bees and wasps to the Community Manager.
  • §7.2.i, vOwner keeps the balcony clean, safe, and waterproofed — liable for water damage caused to the Unit below.
  • §7.3.ivNo cigarette butts, BBQ/shisha, thrown items, large-item storage, or open flames on balconies — unplug appliances when not in use.
  • §7.4.viiItems obstructing Common Areas can be removed and disposed of at the Resident's cost, without prior notice.
  • §7.5Residents must grant access for maintenance on request — advance notice given except in an emergency.
  • §7.6.i–iiUnit interior kept to at least Common Area standard; quarterly AC servicing contract required.
  • §7.6.iiNeighbour leak disputes: Community Manager appoints a third-party investigator — the liable party bears the cost.
  • §7.7.iOwners/Residents must carry contents insurance for full replacement value — the Community Manager's cover does not extend to individual claims.
  • §7.8.vInterior plumbing inspection recommended 2×/year, especially before and after storms.
  • §7.9.iOwner is solely responsible for AC maintenance, interior and exterior units, at their own expense.
MCR §5.1, 2.6

Yards & Landscaping — villa Lots

10 items

This section exists only in the villa-oriented Master Community Rules — towers have no Lots or private gardens, so it's absent from the Vertical Communities document. Included here because the Master Community security team patrols villa street frontages and shared landscaped areas too.

  • MCR §5.1.1Owner keeps all landscaping — shrubs, trees, grass — neatly trimmed and maintained at their own expense, until the Lot's title is transferred.
  • MCR §5.1.2The Board and Community Manager decide, at their discretion, what counts as an acceptable standard of maintenance.
  • MCR §5.1.3Major landscape improvements — irrigation systems, sheds, pergolas, pools, shade structures, fences, gates — need prior Board/Community Manager approval, or removal can be ordered at the Owner's cost.
  • MCR §5.1.4No removing trees from the front or rear garden without the Owners Association's express agreement, regardless of who planted them.
  • MCR §5.1.5No sinking water wells, bore wells, reverse osmosis, or desalination plants anywhere on the property.
  • MCR §5.1.6Dewatering a private swimming pool into the sewer network is strictly prohibited — enforced by NOV and severe penalties, including those set by Dubai Municipality. authority
  • MCR §5.1.7No discharging wastewater or dumping rubbish onto an adjacent plot or directly into the lake.
  • MCR §2.6.5Weeds, rubbish, debris, storage boxes and unsightly items must be regularly cleared from the Lot.
  • MCR §2.6.6Service yards, clotheslines and stored materials on a Lot must be enclosed, fenced or screened so they're not visible from neighbouring property or the street.
  • MCR §2.6.7No incinerator may be kept or maintained on any Lot.
§8

Security & Surveillance

5 items
  • §8.iResidents must take reasonable steps to secure an unoccupied Unit — doors, windows and other openings locked.
  • §8.iiThe Community Manager may enter and secure an unsecured, unoccupied Unit at the Resident's cost.
  • §8.iiiResidents must comply with all posted security protocols: evacuation drills, visitor management, biometric access, parking control.
  • §8.ivCommon Areas are monitored by CCTV; activity there may be recorded as part of the security protocol.
  • §8.vSecurity personnel are authorised to photograph or video violation evidence without needing prior consent from the Resident or Invitee — useful for incident write-ups. authority
§9.1–9.7

Home Appearance

9 items
  • §9.1.i, iiiNo paper/paint/tinfoil on windows; safety screening needs prior written approval and must be translucent.
  • §9.2.i–iiAntennae only where invisible from the street/Common Areas; nothing affixed to the exterior or carport without written approval.
  • §9.3.iNo laundry visible from the street, Common Areas, or lower-level apartments.
  • §9.4.i–iiHoliday lighting needs prior written approval; install ≤10 days before, remove ≤10 days after — LED only.
  • §9.5.i–iiBanners, flags and signage need prior approval. Unapproved Common Area displays are removed immediately; unauthorised in-unit displays are reported to the relevant authorities.
  • §9.6.i–ivNo visible balcony storage; nothing draped over railings; personal items can't exceed the balcony wall height; no combustible storage.
  • §9.7.i–iiNo construction or alteration without submitted plans and prior written approval.
  • §9.7.iiiApproved home-improvement NOC must be displayed on-site during works and kept on file for future Owners.
  • §9.7.viFire alarm devices must be protected during construction or alteration work, to prevent accidental triggering.
§10–11

Service Charges & Compliance

5 items
  • §10.iService Charges (including the Master Community Levy share) are invoiced via the Mollak System.
  • §10.iiiUnpaid Service Charges can mean restricted access to Shared Facilities.
  • §10.ivOn sale/transfer, the new Owner assumes any outstanding Service Charges for that financial year.
  • §11.iOwners, Residents, Holiday Home Operators and their Invitees must comply with the Rules at all times within the Community and Master Community.
  • §11.iiRepeat violations are subject to escalating fines as determined by the Community Manager.
